### About Me

Hi, I'm Max!

I am currently pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science and M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. At WPI, I build electric race cars as part of our [Formula Electric](https://wp.wpi.edu/wpifsae/) electronics team, where I primarily focus on electronics and real-time telemetry. I also build combat robots (AKA Battlebots) and fight with them in tournaments as part of Team WPI. [Check out my post about my 3lb robot, Clu!](https://maxmob.sh/clu-march-nhrl)

I've loved programming as long as I can remember. Something about having a computer do *whatever you want* just really speaks to me. I've been refurbishing, reselling, and hosting servers since I was 12, and using Linux even before then. Eventually, I focused in on programming, and continue to explore many of the different niches of software engineering.

Above all, though, I love thinking about things to make, whether that be software, hardware, or a mix of both. I only began moving beyond just software once I began college, but since then, I've taught myself Solidworks CAD, PCB design & assembly, electronics, manufacturing, and more. My goal with learning these skills is to be able to build any crazy prototype or product I can think up. I am currently working on (and have finished) a variety of projects, some of which are shown above. Please feel free to reach out with questions, opportunities, or anything!
